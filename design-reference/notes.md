# Design references

Human-picked screenshots. Take the **structure**, never the look. DESIGN.md
wins on looks (paper, ink, indigo; Kinfolk and Anthropologie, not a fitness
app). UX.md wins on structure.

## Gentler Streak (`gentler-streak/`), picked September 27, 2026

The owner's words: "pretty good. I still want ours to be more beautiful,
Kinfolk / Anthropologie style, but each screen needs to be clear on what it's
trying to accomplish. Good onboarding with animations. Screenshots of a phone
doing the stuff."

Take:
- **One job per screen, said up top.** A large title names the screen
  ("Activities", "Insights"). Under it, one sentence or one hero element
  that answers "how am I doing?" before anything else (05).
- **One idea per onboarding page:** a big visual on top, a short title, two
  lines of body, and one large button at the bottom (02, 03).
- **Show the phone doing it.** Onboarding shows a real device with the actual
  screen, not a description (04: the Health permission sheet inside a phone;
  03: the watch face with cards floating around it).
- **Name entry as a moment:** a huge, confident text field ("Matt") with one
  arrow button (01).
- **The trust line sits right under the button** that asks for something:
  "Your health data will never leave this device." and the medical
  disclaimer in small gray (04).
- **Explain the core measure visually once**, with a simple annotated scale
  (02: "too low / too high / Daily Condition"). Ours: the indigo scale from
  calm to very rough.
- **Progress:** Week / Month / Year segmented control, one real chart, then
  a short summary, then history. Share is one clear button (06).
- **Warm, first-name copy** ("Hi Matt,", "Fill me in, Matt!").

Leave (it breaks DESIGN.md or our tone):
- Mascot, emoji-like icons, hearts, sparkles, confetti energy.
- Bright filled tiles, a grid of stat tiles, colored cards everywhere.
- "NEW" badges and pills, ALL CAPS labels ("SHARE", "vs. LAST WEEK").
- Streak language and any scoring of the parent.
- Heavy bold sans headlines. Ours are Newsreader: the same size and
  confidence, but editorial.

## Consistency brief, September 27, 2026
A brief from a separate brainstorm session tightened the canvas into one
system: tokens, five type sizes, 12pt corners, one header, one log
control, one summary card, night versions of every tab. Its rules are now
in DESIGN.md and UX.md; the reasoning is in docs/06-decisions.md.
