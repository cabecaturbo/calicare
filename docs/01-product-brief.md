# Product Brief: [APP NAME]

## One line
Your child's skin care plan, organized, and logging that takes one tap.

## Who it's for
Parents of children (roughly 0 to 10) with moderate to severe eczema or TSW. Often exhausted, logging at 2am with a child in one arm, juggling advice from doctors, practitioners, family, and online groups. Many follow detailed plans: prescriptions, or natural protocols with supplements, rotation diets, topical steps, and baths.

## Positioning
"Your plan, organized." The app does not pick sides in the steroid vs. natural debate. It helps families carry out whatever plan their own provider gave them, and gives that provider clean tracking data back.

## The core loop
Log with one tap from anywhere → the app turns logs into simple answers → share progress by text or email in one tap → the family and provider adjust the plan.

## Ways to use it without opening the app
- Interactive Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- Notification buttons ("How was last night?" Good / Okay / Rough)
- Siri phrases, Action Button, Control Center button
- Apple Watch (best for nights: no bright phone)
- iMessage app (drop in a report card)
- Custom keyboard (bonus only; iOS "Full Access" warning scares people)
- Share sheet for images and PDFs; scheduled sends via Shortcuts

## Features by area

**Night:** one-tap itch log, "what worked last time" suggestions, parent sleep tracked too.

**Daily care:** shared routine checklist, topical routine steps, bath rotation, patch-test timer, product diary with "never again" list, restock reminders, photo timeline with ghost overlay.

**Care plan:** import a PDF or photo of the plan; AI turns it into structured items that the parent confirms; flags blanks the provider left. Supplement ramp-up scheduler: one at a time, days apart, start low, auto start/stop dates, split doses, reassess reminders. "Worse since when?" timeline shows what changed before symptoms worsened.

**Food:** safe / testing / paused food list, rotation diet planner (by food family), weekly plant diversity counter, "what can I make tonight" from the fridge, histamine-aware leftovers and freezer timers, food-to-skin timeline, nutrient coverage when food groups are paused.

**Tracking:** rash + itch, sleep, bowel movements, mood, supplement changes. Journal export in the provider's format.

**Sharing:** weekly report card (image), versions for partner, grandma/babysitter, daycare, doctor, and insurance.

**Doctors and insurance:** doctor visit report, treatment history for prior authorization and appeals, refill / prior-auth / copay card reminders, provider message and follow-up tracker.

**Caregivers:** multi-parent sync, shareable care card, daycare medication form reminders.

**Home:** checklist for airing out, HEPA vacuuming, humidity checks.

**For the parent:** monthly "how far you've come" recap. No streaks.

## Money
- Free forever: logging, multiple children, caregiver sync, basic reports.
- Premium (~$5.99/mo or $44.99/yr): insights ("what worked last time"), care plan import, supplement scheduler, meal ideas, full doctor and insurance reports, full photo timeline.
- Affiliate links (labeled): restock reminders, night gear (mittens, cotton/TENCEL sleepwear, bedding), bath and laundry, humidifiers, air purifiers, HEPA vacuums, hygrometers, grocery delivery. Optional filter for NEA Seal of Acceptance products.
- Never: ads, supplement affiliates, "cure" products, links next to treatment decisions.
