# Phase 6: Photos, products, home

Goal: the rest of daily life with eczema: photos, products, the home,
prescriptions and insurance.

Rules (autopilot.md, Part 3): photos never leave the phone (not synced,
not sent to AI). No ads; affiliate links only where a parent is already
buying, always labeled, never next to treatment decisions, never
supplements or "cures". Screens in the phone's style.

---

## 6.1 On-device photo timeline with ghost overlay
1. Take photos of a spot; stored only on the phone (not in the App Group
   database that syncs, not in iCloud Photos unless the parent saves them).
2. A timeline per spot, and a ghost overlay of the last photo when taking
   the next one.
3. Photos appear only in the photo timeline (DESIGN.md §6); reports never
   include them.
Done when: photos work offline and nothing about them syncs (test).

---

## 6.2 Product diary
1. Products used (moisturizers, washes, laundry, clothing), with start
   dates and a "never again" list with the parent's reason.
2. Products join the "Worse since when?" timeline.
Done when: products can be added and marked "never again"; tests pass.

---

## 6.3 Restock reminders and labeled affiliate links
1. Restock reminders for products the parent uses.
2. Affiliate links only in restock and shopping moments, always labeled
   ("Affiliate link"), never next to treatments, never supplements or
   "cures". Optional NEA Seal of Acceptance filter.
3. A setting to turn product links off entirely.
Done when: links show only where allowed (tests check every placement).

---

## 6.4 Home checklist
1. Airing out, HEPA vacuuming, humidity checks, as the plan or parent
   sets them, with reminders. No streaks.
Done when: the checklist runs on the phone.

---

## 6.5 Rx, prior-auth, copay reminders and insurance history report
1. Reminders for refills, prior authorizations, and copay cards.
2. Insurance history report: treatments tried, with dates and outcomes,
   as the parent entered them, for step therapy appeals. PDF, "Not
   medical advice" footer.
Done when: the report builds from real entries and exports; tests pass.
