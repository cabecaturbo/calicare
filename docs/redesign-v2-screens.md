# Redesign v2: what each screen must say in 3 seconds

Phase 2 of the plain-and-obvious redesign (see `06-decisions.md`, October 4, 2026). Each screen answers three questions and leads with one big statement (Newsreader 40pt). Today v2 was approved first; these follow it.

## Onboarding (Welcome → Your child → Reminders → Log from anywhere → Today)

| Screen | What is it? | Why does it help me? | The one thing to do | Big statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Cali Care's first page | Logging takes one tap; the care plan sits in one place | Start | Log itchy nights in one tap. |
| Your child | Naming your child | The app can talk about them by name | Type a first name | Who are we caring for? |
| Reminders | Two short reminders | You don't have to remember to check in | Turn on reminders | Two quiet reminders. |
| Log from anywhere | The Itchy widget | You can log without opening the app, even at 2 AM | Show me how | Log from your Home Screen. |
| Guide step (Home, Lock Screen, Control Center, Action Button) | One step of adding Itchy | Next time, logging is one tap from where you are | Do the step, then Next | The step itself, e.g. "Press and hold an empty spot." |
| Guide: you're set | The end of a guide | It's ready to use | Try it, then Done | You're set. |
| Today, first run | Today with nothing logged yet | Shows where to start | Tap how Cal's skin looks | Start with one tap. |

## Tabs

| Screen | What is it? | Why does it help me? | The one thing to do | Big statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| To do | What to do today, from the care plan | You don't have to remember the plan | Tick the next thing | 3 things left for bedtime. |
| To do, all done | Everything is done | Shows you can rest | Nothing; close the app | All done for today. |
| To do, one step | What one step means | Plain words, plus the doctor's exact words | Read it, then Done | 3 to 4 times a day. |
| How it's going | Is Cal's skin getting better? | See the change over time, and share it with the doctor | Read the statement | Calmer than last week. |
| Care plan | What the doctor told you | Everything from the doctor is in one place | Open a part of the plan | Dr. [NAME]'s plan, from Sep 12. |
| Care plan › Supplements | What to give, and when | Clear about what's given now and what's not yet | Add one to To do (if not giving yet) | Giving 3 supplements. |
| Settings | Change how Cali Care works | Set it up for your family | Pick a row | 2 people help care for Cal. |
| Settings › Quick logging | Places to log from | Log without opening the app | Pick a place | Log without opening the app. |

## Renames (plain words)
- Progress → How it's going. Info → Care plan. Provider → doctor.
- Info › "Care plan" row → "The full plan". "Visits and journal" → "Visits and notes".
- Settings › "Your data" → "Privacy" ("data" is a banned word).
