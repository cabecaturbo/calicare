# To do + Info: what changed

**Before:** `docs/screenshots/before/todo/` (Plan, one long screen).
**After:** this folder, as half-size JPEGs. `-day` is the default text size; `-xxxl` is the largest accessibility size. All shots come from the `LEGACYPLAN` seed (a made-up plan shaped like a real import), with To do's clock pinned to 8:05 AM (morning), 7:40 PM (bedtime), and 9:30 PM with everything ticked (all done).

## Tabs
- Today · To do · Progress · Info · Log. Plan is gone; its two jobs are split.

## To do (`todo-morning-*`, `todo-bedtime-*`, `todo-alldone-*`)
- **One job:** "What do I do right now?"
- **Open block:** shows its name (serif) and "0 of 8 done", then its rows. Other blocks are one row each, with their time or "Done".
- **Rows:** a big circle, a verb-first label ("Put on moisturizer", "Give a bath", "Give Brand D Skin Drops"), and one short line (the dose, or "0 of 3-4 today").
- **Gone:** Start buttons, "New" pills, links, and paragraphs.
- **Skin care** is one counted row instead of three steps per block.
- **All done:** "All done for tonight. Morning list starts at 7:30 AM."
- **Edit** next to the gear is the only other control.

## Item sheet (`todo-skin-sheet-*`)
- "What to do" in plain words, steps numbered.
- Then "Your provider's words" in full.

## Giving question (`todo-ask-*`)
- "Which of these are you giving now?" Asked once per plan. Only checked ones go on To do.

## Info (`info-*`)
- **One job:** "What does the plan say?" Six rows, each with a short detail, each opening its own screen.
- **Supplements:**
  - Giving now, with Morning / Afternoon / Bedtime chips, "How to give", and "Stop giving".
  - Not giving yet, with "Add to To do".
  - The plan's rules.
  - "Your provider mentioned".
- **Patch tests:** "What to do", then "Your provider's words" (the full line, with the last sentence restored), then one "Start a patch test" button.
- **Care plan:** the plan and its baths.

## Words
- Every app-written string passes the grade-6 test.
- Eleven older strings were rewritten, for example "Cali Care keeps the plan your provider gave you."
