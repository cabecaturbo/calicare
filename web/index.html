Why I built Cali Care

My son has topical steroid withdrawal. If you’re reading this, you probably know what that looks like. He wakes up itching, several times a night, scratching his skin until it’s damaged, and crying. All you want is for everyone to get some sleep, and you can’t stop wondering what’s causing it.

I tried writing it down. I tried typing notes on my phone. When you’re sleep deprived, the things that are just a little bit easier make a lot of difference.

So I built the tracker I needed. Logging takes one tap. Cali Care organizes the logs so I can see how he’s actually doing over time. When we see his doctor, I can share it in a way that’s simple to read, instead of trying to remember how the last few weeks went.

I made this for my family. I hope it helps yours.

— Matthew, [TODO: dad / parent] and founder
Progress and sharing

See the pattern, and bring it to the appointment.

Cali Care turns your logs into a picture of how your child has been: nights and skin day by day, how this week compares with last, and everything since your last visit. When something changes, it’s flagged as worth watching.

For your doctor

Before a visit, tap Share with provider to make a short PDF report: a summary, charts, a day-by-day view, and your notes. Send it by AirDrop or email. Your doctor doesn’t need the app.

For your family

Each week, Cali Care also makes a simple card you can text to your partner or family.

Logging at night

Log a rough night without digging through the app.

Add the Cali Care widget to your Home Screen. When your child wakes up, tap Log. The larger widget adds Itchy, Flare, and Note. Every entry saves right away, works offline, and has an Undo button in case you tap by mistake.

You can also log flares (with where on the body), how the night went, skin today, mood, bowel movements, baths, supplements, patch tests, and notes.

Also works with Siri (“Log itching in Cali Care”), the Action Button, and Control Center.

8 PM to 7 AM

Dim and easy to use after 8 PM.

From 8 PM to 7 AM the app switches to warm, dark colors and the buttons get bigger, so you can use it with one hand without lighting up the room.

Daily check-in

One question a day.

The app asks how your child’s skin is today, and one tap answers it. Below that you’ll see a summary of last night and today’s entries.

Calm
A little itchy
Flaring
Very rough
Four levels, darker for worse. There’s no red, and the scale tracks symptoms rather than appearance, so it works on every skin tone.

Turn on a morning check-in and answer Good, Okay, or Rough right from the notification.

Care plan

Your provider’s plan, as a checklist.

Take a photo of the care plan from your doctor or practitioner. Cali Care turns it into morning, afternoon, and bedtime lists in plain language. Every step links back to your provider’s exact words, and nothing is added.

It keeps count of skin-care rounds against the plan (“2 of 3–4 today”) and shows supplements by time of day.

Set a reminder for each list and mark it done from the notification.

Importing a plan requires signing in.

For everyone who helps

Share with your partner.

Invite another caregiver with a code. Each log shows who added it.

Caregiver card.

Make a one-page card for a babysitter or grandparent with what they need to know.

Photo timeline.

Line up photos at the same angle to compare over time. Photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded or added to reports.

What Cali Care won’t do

It follows your plan.

Conventional or natural, Cali Care tracks whatever your provider recommends. It never suggests treatments or makes medical claims.

No streaks.

It never tells you that you missed a day. Pick up whenever you can.

No ads, no data selling.

Cali Care doesn’t track you or sell your data.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone without one. Signing in with Apple lets you sync between phones, share with a partner, and import a care plan.

Where is my child’s data stored?

On your phone, unless you sign in. If you do, your logs sync to our server so they’re on all your phones. Photos always stay on your phone. Deleting your account removes everything from our server, and you can export your data anytime.

Does my doctor need the app?

No. You send them a PDF report.

What happens when I import a care plan?

The text is read on your phone, then sent through our server to Claude, an AI model, which pulls out the steps. Each step links to your provider’s exact words. Cali Care doesn’t add or change anything in your plan.

Is it for TSW or eczema?

Both. I built it for my son, who has TSW.
