Why I built Cali Care

My son struggles with severe eczema. I know what it’s like when your child wakes up several times a night and you desperately want to help, but don’t know how. Keeping track of how he was doing was hard, and we didn’t know what was causing it. So I built something that asks very little of you and helps you work with your provider.

— Matthew, dad and founder

How it helps

Log it.

Tap once from your Home Screen when something happens, even in the middle of the night.

See it.

Cali Care organizes everything, so you can see how your child is doing over time.

Share it.

Send your doctor a clear report, and keep your partner and caregivers in the loop.

Logging

Log a hard night with one tap.

Add the Cali Care widget to your Home Screen. When your child wakes up, tap Log. It saves right away, even offline, and you can undo it.

You can also log flares and where they are, how the night went, mood, baths, supplements, bowel movements, and notes.

You can also use Siri, the Action Button, or Control Center.

Daily check-in

Each day, one question: how is their skin?

Calm
A little itchy
Flaring
Very rough
The scale never uses red. It’s based on symptoms, not how the skin looks, so it works for every skin tone.

You can answer right from an evening reminder.

Progress

Watch the pattern week by week.

Cali Care charts nights and skin day by day, so you can see how this week compares with last. If something changes, it points it out.

Photo timeline

Take photos at the same angle to see changes over time. Your photos stay on your phone.

Care plan

Your provider’s plan, as a checklist.

Take a photo of the plan from your doctor or practitioner. Cali Care turns it into a morning, afternoon, and bedtime checklist, with your provider’s exact words a tap away.

It also keeps count of skin-care rounds, like “2 of 3–4 today,” and shows which supplements to give when.

You’ll need to sign in to import a plan.
Sharing

Everyone who cares for your child, on the same page.

Your doctor

Before a visit, share a PDF report with charts and your notes. Your doctor doesn’t need the app.

Your partner

Invite them with a code. You’ll both see every log and who added it.

Your family

Each week, send a summary card by text.

Babysitters and grandparents

Give them a one-page caregiver card with everything they need to know.

What Cali Care won’t do

It won’t suggest treatments.

It helps you follow the plan from your provider, conventional or natural.

It won’t make you feel behind.

There are no streaks. If you miss a day, pick up where you left off.

It won’t sell your data.

There are no ads and no tracking.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone without one. Sign in with Apple if you want to sync phones, share with a partner, or import a care plan.

Where is my child’s data stored?

On your phone. If you sign in, your logs also sync to our server so they’re on all your devices. Photos always stay on your phone, and you can export your data or delete your account at any time.

Does my doctor need the app?

No. You send them a PDF report by email or AirDrop.

What happens when I import a care plan?

The plan’s text goes through our server to Claude, an AI model, which turns it into steps. Each step links back to your provider’s exact words, and nothing is added.

Free during the beta.

Available on iPhone, and it works offline.
