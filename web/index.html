Why I built Cali Care

My son struggles with severe eczema. I know what it’s like when your child wakes up several times a night and you desperately want to help, but don’t know how. Keeping track of how he was doing was hard, and we didn’t know what was causing it. So I built something that asks very little of you and helps you work with your provider.

— Matthew, dad and founder
Progress and sharing

See how this week compares with last.

Nights and skin by day, week over week, and since the last visit. Changes get flagged as worth watching.

For your doctor

A PDF report with charts, a day-by-day view, and your notes. They don’t need the app.

For your family

A weekly card you can text.

Logging at night

Log from your Home Screen.

Tap Log when they wake up. It saves instantly, works offline, and can be undone.

Also log: flares and where, nights, skin, mood, bowel movements, baths, supplements, patch tests, notes.

Also works with Siri (“Log itching in Cali Care”), the Action Button, and Control Center.

Care plan

Your provider’s plan, as a checklist.

Photograph the plan. Cali Care sorts it into morning, afternoon, and bedtime, and every step links to your provider’s exact words.

Counts skin-care rounds (“2 of 3–4 today”) and shows supplements by time of day.

Plan import requires sign-in.

For everyone who helps

Share with your partner.

Invite them with a code. Each log shows who added it.

Caregiver card.

One page for the babysitter or grandparents.

Photo timeline.

Compare photos at the same angle. They never leave your phone.

What Cali Care won’t do

Suggest treatments.

It follows the plan your provider gave you, conventional or natural.

Guilt you.

No streaks, and it never says you missed a day.

Sell your data.

No ads, no tracking.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in with Apple to sync phones, share with a partner, or import a plan.

Where is my child’s data stored?

On your phone, unless you sign in; then logs sync to our server. Photos never leave your phone, and you can export your data or delete your account anytime.

Does my doctor need the app?

No. You send them a PDF.

What happens when I import a care plan?

The text goes through our server to Claude, an AI model, which pulls out the steps. Each one links to your provider’s exact words.

Is it for TSW or eczema?

Both. I built it for my son.

Free during the beta.

iPhone only. Works offline.
