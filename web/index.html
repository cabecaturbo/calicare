Cali Care

Your child's eczema plan, one tap at a time.

Cali Care keeps the plan your provider gave you and a simple log in one place, so you can follow it without opening an app at 2 AM.

Made for tired hands

  • One tap to log. From a widget, the Lock Screen, Siri, or the Action Button.
  • Your plan, in plain words. Bring in your provider's plan and see what to do now, morning to bedtime.
  • See how things go. Nights, skin, and changes, compared only with your child's own weeks.
  • Share with family and your doctor. Everyone sees the same day. A tidy report for visits.

Calm by design

No streaks. No guilt. No red for a bad day. Photos of your child never leave your phone.

Cali Care never suggests treatments and does not diagnose. It organizes the plan you already have. Not medical advice.
