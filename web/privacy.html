Cali Care

Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Cali Care is made by CursorKittens LLC. It holds private things about your family, so we keep as little as we can, and most of it never leaves your phone.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • Photos and the care plan files you bring in never leave your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can export your logs and delete your account at any time.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log, your children's names, your routine, and your care plan are stored on your phone. Cali Care's widgets and Siri read from the same place. Photos never leave your phone. They aren't synced, backed up by us, or sent anywhere. The original care plan file (a photo or PDF) also stays on your phone.

If you sign in

An account is optional. You sign in with Apple, so we get only what Apple shares: an account ID and the email you choose (which can be Apple's private relay address). You pick the name others in your family see, like "Mom" or "Grandpa".

When you're signed in, these are copied to our server so your family sees the same day on every phone:

  • Your children's names, and birth dates if you add them
  • Your logs (for example itching, nights, skin, flares, notes) and who logged them
  • Your routine steps
  • The care plan's items and provider visits
  • Your food list and product diary

Only people you invite to your family can see this. Our server is run by Supabase, in the United States, and the data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Reading your care plan

When you bring in a care plan, Cali Care can read it for you and list the steps in plain words. This needs an account. The plan's text is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude to pull out the steps. We don't store the text on our server; we only count how many plans you read each day, to keep things fair. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this text is not used to train their models.

What we never do

  • No ads, and no ad or analytics tracking.
  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use your family's information to train AI.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports.

Children

Cali Care is for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. A child's information is entered by their parent or caregiver, who controls it and can delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › Your data gives you every log as a spreadsheet.
  • Sign out: everything stays on your phone, and syncing stops.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account and your data from our server. If you're the last owner of your family group, the whole family's data is removed. To clear the phone too, delete the app.

Not medical advice

Cali Care organizes the plan your provider gave you. It never suggests treatments and does not diagnose.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if the change matters. Questions? Write to msmccartin@gmail.com.
